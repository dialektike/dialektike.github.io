"To know is to have a belief that tracks the truth."

2013-11-26

“안다는 것은 그 진리를 추적하는 믿음을 갖는 것이다(To know is to have a belief that tracks the truth),” p.178, Nozick, R., (1981) Philosophical Explanations, Harvard University Press.
